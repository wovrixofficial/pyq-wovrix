Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

At Learnosha, student privacy is an uncompromised engineering priority. This policy outlines our data practices in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India) and international privacy frameworks.

1. Local-First Architecture

All student progress features—including question bookmarks, completed questions, custom paper generator presets, and theme preferences—are stored strictly within your browser's local memory (localStorage). We do not transmit or store your personal study history on external servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT require user account registration, names, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade student usage data to coaching institutes or third-party advertisers.
  • We do NOT use cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Voluntary Feedback & Error Reports

When you submit an error report via the 🚩 Report button on a question card, the submission transmits only the Question ID, selected issue categories, optional student notes, and a timestamp to assist our academic review team.

4. Third-Party Hosting & Content Delivery

Our static assets and datasets are delivered via Vercel Global Edge Network and Cloudflare CDN. These providers process standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses and browser User-Agent strings) strictly for DDoS prevention, rate limiting, and network delivery security.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, contact us at privacy@learnosha.in.
