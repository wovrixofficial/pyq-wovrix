Assertion (A) : Actinoids show wide range of oxidation states. Reason (R) : Actinoids are radioactive in nature.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Correct Option: (b) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true, but Reason (R) is not the correct explanation of Assertion (A).

Step 1 (Conceptual Basis): [0.5 Mark]

The Assertion (A) is true because actinoids exhibit a wide range of oxidation states (e.g., $+3, +4, +5, +6, +7$) due to the comparable energies of the $5f$, $6d$, and $7s$ orbitals. This allows electrons from all these subshells to participate in bonding.

Step 2 (Analysis of Reason): [0.25 Mark]

The Reason (R) is true because all actinoids are indeed radioactive. However, radioactivity is a property of the unstable nucleus, whereas the oxidation state is a property of the electronic configuration and the energy levels of the valence electrons.

Step 3 (Final Conclusion): [0.25 Mark]

Since the radioactivity of the nucleus does not determine the ability of electrons to participate in chemical bonding or the distribution of oxidation states, the Reason (R) does not explain the Assertion (A). Therefore, the correct choice is (b).

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the oxidation state like a person having many different "outfits" (energy levels) they can wear because their closet (the $5f$, $6d$, and $7s$ orbitals) is very messy and all the clothes are easy to reach. Being radioactive just means the person is "shaky" or unstable, which has nothing to do with how many outfits they can pick from their closet!
