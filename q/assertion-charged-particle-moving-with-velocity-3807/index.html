Physics Moving Charges and Magnetism 1 Mark
Appeared in: 2025 (3 sets)
Assertion (A) : A charged particle is moving with velocity $v$ in x-y plane, making an angle $\theta$ ($0 < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}$) with x-axis. If a uniform magnetic field $\vec{B}$ is applied in the region, along y-axis, the particle will move in a helical path with its axis parallel to x-axis. Reason (R) : The direction of the magnetic force acting on a charged particle moving in a magnetic field is along the velocity of the particle.
