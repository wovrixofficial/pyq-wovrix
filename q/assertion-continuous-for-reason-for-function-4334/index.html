**Assertion (A):** $f(x) = \begin{cases} 3x - 8, & x \le 5 \\ 2k, & x > 5 \end{cases}$ is continuous at $x = 5$ for $k = \frac{5}{2}$. **Reason (R):** For a function f to be continuous at $x = a$, $\lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) = \lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = f(a)$.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Assertion (A): $f(x) = \begin{cases} 3x - 8, & x \le 5 \\ 2k, & x > 5 \end{cases}$ is continuous at $x = 5$ for $k = \frac{5}{2}$.

Reason (R): For a function $f$ to be continuous at $x = a$, $\lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) = \lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = f(a)$.

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1/3 Mark]

A function $f(x)$ is continuous at $x = a$ if and only if the Left Hand Limit (LHL), Right Hand Limit (RHL), and the value of the function at that point are equal:

$$\lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) = \lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = f(a)$$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1/3 Mark]

  • LHL at $x=5$: $\lim_{x \to 5^-} f(x) = \lim_{x \to 5} (3x - 8) = 3(5) - 8 = 15 - 8 = 7$
  • Value at $x=5$: $f(5) = 3(5) - 8 = 7$
  • RHL at $x=5$: $\lim_{x \to 5^+} f(x) = \lim_{x \to 5} (2k) = 2k$

For continuity, LHL = RHL = $f(5)$:

$$7 = 2k \implies k = \frac{7}{2}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1/3 Mark]

Since the calculated value $k = \frac{7}{2}$ does not match the given value $k = \frac{5}{2}$ in Assertion (A), the Assertion is False. However, the Reason (R) is the standard definition of continuity, which is True.

Correct Option: Assertion (A) is false, but Reason (R) is true.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Imagine a bridge made of two parts; for the bridge to be safe, the left side and the right side must meet at the exact same height as the middle point. Here, the math shows the left side ends at height 7, so the right side must also be 7, but the problem incorrectly claimed it should be 5.
