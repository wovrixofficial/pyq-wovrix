Mathematics Three-Dimensional Geometry 1 Mark
Appeared in: 2023 (3 sets)
Assertion (A) : If a line makes angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ with positive direction of the coordinate axes, then $\sin^2 \alpha + \sin^2 \beta + \sin^2 \gamma = 2$. Reason (R) : The sum of squares of the direction cosines of a line is $1$.
