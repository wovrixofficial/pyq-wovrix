Assertion (A) : The ratio of the speeds of the electron in the second to that in the third orbit in Bohr's hydrogen atom is $\frac{3}{2}$. Reason (R) : The speed of the electron in $\text{n}^{\text{th}}$ permitted orbit in hydrogen atom varies proportional to $\frac{1}{\text{n}}$ i.e. $v_{\text{n}} \propto \frac{1}{\text{n}}$.
