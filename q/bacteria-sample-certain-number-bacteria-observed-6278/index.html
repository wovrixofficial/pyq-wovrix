Mathematics Differential Equations 4 Marks
Appeared in: 2024 (2 sets)
A bacteria sample of certain number of bacteria is observed to grow exponentially in a given amount of time. Using exponential growth model, the rate of growth of this sample of bacteria is calculated. The differential equation representing the growth of bacteria is given as : $\frac{\text{dP}}{\text{dt}} = \text{kP}$, where P is the population of bacteria at any time 't'.
