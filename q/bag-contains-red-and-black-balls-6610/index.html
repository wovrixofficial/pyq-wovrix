Bag I contains 3 red and 4 black balls, Bag II contains 5 red and 2 black balls. Two balls are transferred at random from Bag I to Bag II and then a ball is drawn at random from Bag II. Find the probability that the drawn ball is red in colour.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

CBSE Class 12 Mathematics: Probability Marking Scheme

Step 1 (Defining Events and Probabilities): [1 Mark]

Let $E_1, E_2, E_3$ be the events of transferring balls from Bag I to Bag II:

  • $E_1$: 2 Red balls are transferred.
  • $E_2$: 1 Red and 1 Black ball are transferred.
  • $E_3$: 2 Black balls are transferred.

Bag I has 3 Red, 4 Black (Total 7). The probabilities of transfer are:

$$P(E_1) = \frac{\binom{3}{2}}{\binom{7}{2}} = \frac{3}{21}, \quad P(E_2) = \frac{\binom{3}{1}\binom{4}{1}}{\binom{7}{2}} = \frac{12}{21}, \quad P(E_3) = \frac{\binom{4}{2}}{\binom{7}{2}} = \frac{6}{21}$$

Step 2 (Conditional Probabilities and Total Probability Theorem): [3 Marks]

Let $A$ be the event that the ball drawn from Bag II (now containing 5+2=7 balls + 2 transferred = 9 balls) is Red.

  • If $E_1$ occurs, Bag II has (5+2) Red and 2 Black = 7 Red, 2 Black. $P(A|E_1) = \frac{7}{9}$
  • If $E_2$ occurs, Bag II has (5+1) Red and (2+1) Black = 6 Red, 3 Black. $P(A|E_2) = \frac{6}{9}$
  • If $E_3$ occurs, Bag II has 5 Red and (2+2) Black = 5 Red, 4 Black. $P(A|E_3) = \frac{5}{9}$

By the Law of Total Probability:

$$P(A) = P(E_1)P(A|E_1) + P(E_2)P(A|E_2) + P(E_3)P(A|E_3)$$ $$P(A) = \left( \frac{3}{21} \times \frac{7}{9} \right) + \left( \frac{12}{21} \times \frac{6}{9} \right) + \left( \frac{6}{21} \times \frac{5}{9} \right)$$

Step 3 (Final Calculation): [1 Mark]

$$P(A) = \frac{21 + 72 + 30}{189} = \frac{123}{189}$$

Simplifying by dividing by 3:

$$P(A) = \frac{41}{63}$$

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of this like a "weighted average." Since we don't know exactly which balls moved, we calculate the chance of every possible move happening and multiply it by how likely we are to pick a red ball in that specific scenario, then add them all up to get the final answer.
