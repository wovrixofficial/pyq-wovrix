Question: Find $\frac{dS}{dx}$ for the surface area $S$ of a closed cuboid with a square base of side $x$ and height $h$, given that the volume $V$ is constant.

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

The surface area $S$ of a closed cuboid with a square base of side $x$ and height $h$ is given by:

$$S = 2x^2 + 4xh$$

The volume $V$ of the cuboid is constant and is given by:

$$V = x^2h \implies h = \frac{V}{x^2}$$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [3 Marks]

Substitute the expression for $h$ into the surface area formula to express $S$ as a function of $x$ only:

$$S = 2x^2 + 4x\left(\frac{V}{x^2}\right)$$ $$S = 2x^2 + \frac{4V}{x}$$

Now, differentiate $S$ with respect to $x$ using the power rule $\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}$:

$$\frac{dS}{dx} = \frac{d}{dx}(2x^2) + \frac{d}{dx}(4Vx^{-1})$$ $$\frac{dS}{dx} = 2(2x) + 4V(-1x^{-2})$$ $$\frac{dS}{dx} = 4x - \frac{4V}{x^2}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1 Mark]

The final derivative representing the rate of change of surface area with respect to the side length $x$ is:

$$\frac{dS}{dx} = 4x - \frac{4V}{x^2}$$

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of this like balancing a budget; since the volume is fixed, if you make the base wider, the box must get shorter to keep the same amount of space inside, and this formula simply tracks how that trade-off changes the total amount of cardboard needed for the surface.
