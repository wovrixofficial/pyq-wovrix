Find the net electric field $\vec{E}$ at point $\vec{r} = (3\text{ m})\hat{i} + (4\text{ m})\hat{j}$.

Part (b)(i): Current in a Conductor of Varying Cross-section

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

  • Given: $I = 1\text{ A}$, $n = 8.5 \times 10^{28}\text{ m}^{-3}$, $A_A = 1.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}^2$, $A_B = 2.0 \times 10^{-7}\text{ m}^2$.
  • Ohm's Law in terms of current density: $J = \sigma E = \frac{I}{A} \implies E = \frac{I}{\sigma A}$.
  • Drift velocity formula: $I = neA v_d \implies v_d = \frac{I}{neA}$.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1.5 Marks]

(I) Ratio of Electric Fields:

$$ \frac{E_A}{E_B} = \frac{I / (\sigma A_A)}{I / (\sigma A_B)} = \frac{A_B}{A_A} $$ $$ \frac{E_A}{E_B} = \frac{2.0 \times 10^{-7}}{1.0 \times 10^{-7}} = 2 $$

(II) Drift velocity at B:

$$ v_{d,B} = \frac{I}{ne A_B} = \frac{1}{(8.5 \times 10^{28})(1.6 \times 10^{-19})(2.0 \times 10^{-7})} $$ $$ v_{d,B} = \frac{1}{27.2 \times 10^2} = \frac{1}{2720} \approx 3.68 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m/s} $$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

  • Ratio $E_A : E_B = 2 : 1$.
  • Drift velocity at B $\approx 3.68 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m/s}$.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the wire like a water pipe; if the pipe gets wider, the water has to slow down to keep the same amount flowing through, and the "push" (electric field) needed to move the charges changes based on how crowded the path is.
