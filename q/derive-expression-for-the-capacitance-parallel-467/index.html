Physics Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance 5 Marks
Appeared in: 2026 (3 sets)
(a) Derive an expression for the capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor of plate area A and plate separation d with air present between the plates. (b) Two air-filled capacitors of capacitances $C_1$ and $C_2$ are connected in parallel with a dc battery. After the capacitors are fully charged, a slab of dielectric constant K is inserted between the plates of each capacitor. How will the (i) charge on each capacitor and (ii) energy stored in the capacitor affected after the slab is introduced. OR (a) An electric field $\vec{E}$ is established across the ends of a cylindrical conductor of length L and area of cross-section A. Discuss how electrons attain an average velocity, independent of time. Hence, obtain a relation between current in the conductor and this 'average velocity' of electrons.
