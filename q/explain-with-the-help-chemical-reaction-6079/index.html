Chemistry Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids 5 Marks
Appeared in: 2024 (3 sets)
(a) (i) Explain with the help of chemical reaction when: (I) Acetone is treated with semicarbazide. (II) Two molecules of benzaldehyde are treated with conc. $\text{NaOH}$. (III) Butan-2-one is treated with $\text{Zn/Hg}$ and conc. $\text{HCl}$. (ii) Arrange the following in the increasing order of their acidic strength: (I) $\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{COOH}$, $\text{BrCH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{CH}_2\text{COOH}$, $\text{CH}_3\text{CHBrCH}_2\text{COOH}$, $\text{CH}_3\text{CH}_2\text{CHBrCOOH}$ (II) Benzoic acid, 4-Methoxybenzoic acid, 4-Nitrobenzoic acid, 3,4-Dinitrobenzoic acid
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

CBSE Class 12 Chemistry: Marking Scheme Solution

(a) (i) Chemical Reactions [3 Marks]

  • (I) Acetone with Semicarbazide [1 Mark]: Nucleophilic addition-elimination reaction forming a semicarbazone. $$CH_3COCH_3 + NH_2NHCONH_2 \rightarrow CH_3C(NNHCONH_2)CH_3 + H_2O$$
  • (II) Two molecules of Benzaldehyde with conc. NaOH [1 Mark]: Cannizzaro reaction (disproportionation). $$2C_6H_5CHO + conc. NaOH \rightarrow C_6H_5CH_2OH + C_6H_5COONa$$
  • (III) Butan-2-one with Zn/Hg and conc. HCl [1 Mark]: Clemmensen reduction. $$CH_3COCH_2CH_3 \xrightarrow{Zn/Hg, conc. HCl} CH_3CH_2CH_2CH_3 + H_2O$$

(a) (ii) Increasing Order of Acidic Strength [2 Marks]

Step 1 (Governing Principle): [0.5 Mark] Acidic strength is directly proportional to the stability of the conjugate base (carboxylate ion). Electron-withdrawing groups (EWG) increase acidity by dispersing negative charge via the inductive effect (-I effect), while electron-donating groups (EDG) decrease it.

Step 2 (Analysis of Substituents): [1 Mark]

  • (I) Aliphatic Acids: The -I effect of Br is stronger when closer to the -COOH group.
    Order: $CH_3CH_2CH_2COOH < BrCH_2CH_2CH_2COOH < CH_3CHBrCH_2COOH < CH_3CH_2CHBrCOOH$
  • (II) Benzoic Acid Derivatives: Nitro groups (-NO$_2$) are EWG (increase acidity), while Methoxy groups (-OCH$_3$) are EDG (decrease acidity).
    Order: $4\text{-Methoxybenzoic acid} < \text{Benzoic acid} < 4\text{-Nitrobenzoic acid} < 3,4\text{-Dinitrobenzoic acid}$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The increasing order is determined by the proximity and number of electron-withdrawing groups.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the -COOH group as a "proton-releasing" engine; if you attach "electron-hungry" atoms (like Br or NO$_2$) nearby, they pull electrons away, making it much easier for the engine to let go of that H+ ion, thus making the acid stronger!
