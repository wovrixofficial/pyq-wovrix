(a) Find the equation of a line passing through the point $(1, -1, 2)$ and parallel to x-axis. Also, find the shortest distance between this line and the line $\vec{r} = 2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k} + \mu$3\hat{i} - 5\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$$्ले. OR (b) Find the equations of the lines AC and BD, if coordinates of points A, B, C and D respectively, are $(3, -4, 2), (-1, -2, 0), (4, 1, -1)$ and $(4, 0, 5)$. Show that the lines AC and BD are perpendicular to each other.

Solution for Question (a)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

  • The equation of a line passing through point $\vec{a}(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and parallel to vector $\vec{b}$ is given by $\vec{r} = \vec{a} + \lambda \vec{b}$.
  • Since the line is parallel to the x-axis, its direction vector is $\vec{b} = \hat{i} + 0\hat{j} + 0\hat{k}$.
  • Given point $A(1, -1, 2)$, so $\vec{a} = \hat{i} - \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$.
  • Shortest distance formula between two lines $\vec{r} = \vec{a}_1 + \lambda \vec{b}_1$ and $\vec{r} = \vec{a}_2 + \mu \vec{b}_2$: $$d = \left| \frac{(\vec{a}_2 - \vec{a}_1) \cdot (\vec{b}_1 \times \vec{b}_2)}{|\vec{b}_1 \times \vec{b}_2|} \right|$$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1.5 Marks]

  • Equation of the line: $\vec{r} = (\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}) + \lambda(\hat{i})$.
  • Shortest Distance Calculation: $\vec{a}_1 = \hat{i} - \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$, $\vec{b}_1 = \hat{i}$ $\vec{a}_2 = 2\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}$, $\vec{b}_2 = 3\hat{i} - 5\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$ $\vec{a}_2 - \vec{a}_1 = (2-1)\hat{i} + (1 - (-1))\hat{j} + (-1-2)\hat{k} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}$ $\vec{b}_1 \times \vec{b}_2 = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 3 & -5 & 2 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i}(0) - \hat{j}(2) + \hat{k}(-5) = -2\hat{j} - 5\hat{k}$ $|\vec{b}_1 \times \vec{b}_2| = \sqrt{(-2)^2 + (-5)^2} = \sqrt{4 + 25} = \sqrt{29}$ $(\vec{a}_2 - \vec{a}_1) \cdot (\vec{b}_1 \times \vec{b}_2) = (\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}) \cdot (-2\hat{j} - 5\hat{k}) = 0 - 4 + 15 = 11$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The shortest distance is $\frac{11}{\sqrt{29}}$ units.
