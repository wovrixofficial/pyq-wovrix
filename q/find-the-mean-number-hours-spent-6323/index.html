Question: Find the mean number of hours spent by the student.

Step 1 (Formula & Given Data): [1 Mark]

To find the mean ($\bar{x}$) of a grouped frequency distribution, we use the Direct Method formula:

$$\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{\sum f_i}$$

Where:

  • $x_i$ = Class mark (mid-point) of each interval, calculated as $\frac{\text{Upper Limit} + \text{Lower Limit}}{2}$.
  • $f_i$ = Frequency corresponding to each class interval.
  • $\sum f_i$ = Total number of observations.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [3 Marks]

Construct the frequency distribution table to calculate $f_i x_i$:

Class Interval Frequency ($f_i$) Class Mark ($x_i$) $f_i x_i$
0-2 $f_1$ 1 $1f_1$
2-4 $f_2$ 3 $3f_2$
4-6 $f_3$ 5 $5f_3$
Total $\sum f_i$ - $\sum f_i x_i$

Substitute the values into the summation:

$$\bar{x} = \frac{(f_1 \times 1) + (f_2 \times 3) + (f_3 \times 5) + \dots}{\sum f_i}$$

(Note: In a board examination, ensure all arithmetic operations are shown clearly. If specific data values were provided in the prompt, substitute them here to arrive at the numerical sum.)

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1 Mark]

After performing the summation and division:

$$\bar{x} = \text{Result Value}$$

The mean number of hours spent by the student is [Result] hours.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the mean as the "balance point" of your data; by multiplying each group's middle value by how many times it happens, you find the total "weight" of the data, which you then spread out evenly across all students to find the average.
