(a) Find the probability distribution of the number of boys in families having three children, assuming equal probability for a boy and a girl. OR (b) A coin is tossed twice. Let X be a random variable defined as number of heads minus number of tails. Obtain the probability distribution of X and also find its mean.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution for Part (b): Probability Distribution and Mean

Step 1 (Definition of Random Variable and Sample Space): [1 Mark]

A coin is tossed twice. The sample space $S$ is given by:

$$S = \{HH, HT, TH, TT\}$$

The random variable $X$ is defined as (Number of Heads) - (Number of Tails). We calculate $X$ for each outcome:

  • For $HH$: $X = 2 - 0 = 2$
  • For $HT$: $X = 1 - 1 = 0$
  • For $TH$: $X = 1 - 1 = 0$
  • For $TT$: $X = 0 - 2 = -2$

Step 2 (Probability Distribution Table): [2 Marks]

Since the coin is fair, each outcome has a probability of $\frac{1}{4}$. We group the values of $X$ and sum their probabilities:

  • $P(X = -2) = P(\{TT\}) = \frac{1}{4}$
  • $P(X = 0) = P(\{HT, TH\}) = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$
  • $P(X = 2) = P(\{HH\}) = \frac{1}{4}$

The probability distribution table is:

$X$ $-2$ $0$ $2$
$P(X)$ $\frac{1}{4}$ $\frac{1}{2}$ $\frac{1}{4}$

Step 3 (Calculation of Mean): [2 Marks]

The mean $E(X)$ (or $\mu$) is calculated using the formula:

$$E(X) = \sum x_i P(x_i)$$

Substituting the values from the distribution:

$$E(X) = (-2) \times \left(\frac{1}{4}\right) + (0) \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) + (2) \times \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)$$ $$E(X) = -\frac{2}{4} + 0 + \frac{2}{4}$$ $$E(X) = 0$$

💡 EL10 Concept Insight:

Think of the mean as the "balance point" of the game; because the chance of getting more heads is exactly the same as getting more tails, they cancel each other out perfectly, leaving the average result at zero.
