CBSE Class 12 Mathematics: Shortest Distance Between Two Lines

Step 1 (Formula & Given Data): [1 Mark]

The shortest distance $d$ between two skew lines $\vec{r} = \vec{a_1} + \lambda\vec{b_1}$ and $\vec{r} = \vec{a_2} + \mu\vec{b_2}$ is given by the formula:

$$d = \left| \frac{(\vec{a_2} - \vec{a_1}) \cdot (\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2})}{|\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2}|} \right|$$

Given Data:

  • For $L_1$: Point $\vec{a_1} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}$, Direction $\vec{b_1} = \hat{i} + \hat{j} + 3\hat{k}$
  • For $L_2$: $\vec{r} = (\hat{i} + 0\hat{j} - 2\hat{k}) + \mu(0\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 1\hat{k})$. Thus, $\vec{a_2} = \hat{i} - 2\hat{k}$ and $\vec{b_2} = 2\hat{j} - \hat{k}$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [3 Marks]

(i) Calculate $(\vec{a_2} - \vec{a_1})$:

$$\vec{a_2} - \vec{a_1} = (\hat{i} - 2\hat{k}) - (2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}) = -\hat{i} + \hat{j} - 3\hat{k}$$

(ii) Calculate cross product $\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2}$:

$$\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2} = \begin{vmatrix} \hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\ 1 & 1 & 3 \\ 0 & 2 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = \hat{i}(-1 - 6) - \hat{j}(-1 - 0) + \hat{k}(2 - 0) = -7\hat{i} + \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$$

(iii) Calculate magnitude $|\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2}|$:

$$|\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2}| = \sqrt{(-7)^2 + (1)^2 + (2)^2} = \sqrt{49 + 1 + 4} = \sqrt{54} = 3\sqrt{6}$$

(iv) Calculate dot product $(\vec{a_2} - \vec{a_1}) \cdot (\vec{b_1} \times \vec{b_2})$:

$$(-1\hat{i} + 1\hat{j} - 3\hat{k}) \cdot (-7\hat{i} + 1\hat{j} + 2\hat{k}) = (-1)(-7) + (1)(1) + (-3)(2) = 7 + 1 - 6 = 2$$
