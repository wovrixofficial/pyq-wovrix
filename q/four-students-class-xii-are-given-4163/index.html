(a) Four students of class XII are given a problem to solve independently. Their chances of solving the problem respectively are $\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{1}{5}$. Find the probability that at most one of them will solve the problem.\nOR\n(b) The probability distribution of a random variable X is given below :\n\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}\hline\text{X} & 1 & 2 & 4 & 2k & 3k & 5k \\\hline\text{P(X)} & $\frac{1}{2}$ & $\frac{1}{5}$ & $\frac{3}{25}$ & $\frac{1}{10}$ & $\frac{1}{25}$ & $\frac{1}{25}$ \\\hline\end{tabular}\nFind k, if $\text{E(X)} = 2 \cdot 94$ and also find $\text{P(X} \le 4)$.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution for Part (a)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

Let $A, B, C,$ and $D$ be the events that the four students solve the problem. The given probabilities are:

  • $P(A) = \frac{1}{2} \implies P(A') = \frac{1}{2}$
  • $P(B) = \frac{1}{3} \implies P(B') = \frac{2}{3}$
  • $P(C) = \frac{2}{3} \implies P(C') = \frac{1}{3}$
  • $P(D) = \frac{1}{5} \implies P(D') = \frac{4}{5}$

The probability that at most one solves the problem is given by: $P(\text{None solve}) + P(\text{Exactly one solves})$.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [3 Marks]

Case 1: None solve the problem

$$P(\text{None}) = P(A')P(B')P(C')P(D') = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{90} = \frac{4}{45}$$

Case 2: Exactly one solves the problem

  • Only A: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{90}$
  • Only B: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{4}{90}$
  • Only C: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{16}{90}$
  • Only D: $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{5} = \frac{2}{90}$
$$P(\text{Exactly one}) = \frac{8+4+16+2}{90} = \frac{30}{90} = \frac{1}{3}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1 Mark]

$$P(\text{At most one}) = P(\text{None}) + P(\text{Exactly one}) = \frac{4}{45} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4+15}{45} = \frac{19}{45}$$

Solution for Part (b)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

The expectation $E(X) = \sum X_i P(X_i)$. Given $E(X) = 2.94$.

$$E(X) = (1 \cdot \frac{1}{2}) + (2 \cdot \frac{1}{5}) + (4 \cdot \frac{3}{25}) + (2k \cdot \frac{1}{10}) + (3k \cdot \frac{1}{25}) + (5k \cdot \frac{1}{25})$$
