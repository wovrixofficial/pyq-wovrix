(a) 10 identical blocks are marked with '0' on two of them, '1' on three of them, '2' on four of them and '3' on one of them and put in a box. If $X$ denotes the number written on the block, then write the probability distribution of $X$ and calculate its mean. OR (b) In a village of 8000 people, 3000 go out of the village to work and 4000 are women. It is noted that $30%$ of women go out of the village to work. What is the probability that a randomly chosen individual is either a woman or a person working outside the village?
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution (a): Probability Distribution and Mean

Step 1 (Foundational Data & Probability Definition): [0.5 Mark]

Total number of blocks $N = 10$. The random variable $X$ takes values $\{0, 1, 2, 3\}$. The probability $P(X=x)$ is given by $\frac{\text{Number of blocks with } x}{\text{Total blocks}}$.

  • $P(X=0) = \frac{2}{10} = 0.2$
  • $P(X=1) = \frac{3}{10} = 0.3$
  • $P(X=2) = \frac{4}{10} = 0.4$
  • $P(X=3) = \frac{1}{10} = 0.1$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1 Mark]

The probability distribution table is:

$X$0123
$P(X)$0.20.30.40.1

The mean $E(X)$ is calculated using the formula $E(X) = \sum x_i P(x_i)$:

$$E(X) = (0 \times 0.2) + (1 \times 0.3) + (2 \times 0.4) + (3 \times 0.1)$$ $$E(X) = 0 + 0.3 + 0.8 + 0.3$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The mean of the distribution is:

$$E(X) = 1.4$$

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the mean as a "weighted average"—it’s like finding the balance point on a seesaw where the heavier weights (more frequent numbers) pull the average closer to them.

Solution (b): Probability of Union of Events

Step 1 (Formula & Given Data): [0.5 Mark]

Let $W$ be the event that the person is a woman, and $O$ be the event that the person works outside. We need $P(W \cup O) = P(W) + P(O) - P(W \cap O)$.

  • Total people $n(S) = 8000$
  • $n(W) = 4000 \implies P(W) = \frac{4000}{8000} = 0.5$
  • $n(O) = 3000 \implies P(O) = \frac{3000}{8000} = 0.375$
  • $n(W \cap O) = 30\% \text{ of } 4000 = 0.3 \times 4000 = 1200 \implies P(W \cap O) = \frac{1200}{8000} = 0.15$
