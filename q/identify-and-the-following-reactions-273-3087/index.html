Chemistry Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids 5 Marks

(a) (i) Identify A, B and C in the following reactions : $$\text{Toluene} + \text{CrO}_3 \xrightarrow{(\text{CH}_3\text{CO})_2\text{O}}{273-283\text{ K}} \text{A} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \text{B}$$ $$\text{B} \xrightarrow{\text{Conc. NaOH}} \text{C} + \text{Sodium benzoate}$$ (ii) Give reasons for the following : (I) Carboxylic acids do not give the characteristic reactions of carbonyl group. (II) Ethanoic acid is a stronger acid than ethanol. OR (b) (i) Write the product(s) in the following reactions : (I) $2\text{CH}_3\text{COOH} \xrightarrow[\text{heat}]{\text{P}_4\text{O}_{10}}$ (II) Benzoyl chloride + $(\text{CH}_3)_2\text{Cd} \rightarrow$ (III) Phthalamide $\xrightarrow{\text{strong heating}}$ (ii) Write the reaction involved in the following reactions : (I) Wolff-Kishner Reduction (II) Decarboxylation Reaction
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

(a) (i) Identification of A, B, and C

Step 1 (Reaction Identification): [1.5 Marks]

  • The first reaction is the Etard Reaction. Toluene reacts with chromyl chloride or $\text{CrO}_3$ in acetic anhydride to form a gem-diacetate intermediate.
  • A: Benzylidene diacetate, $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}(\text{OCOCH}_3)_2$
  • B: Benzaldehyde, $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CHO}$ (formed by hydrolysis of A).
  • C: Benzyl alcohol, $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_2\text{OH}$ (formed by Cannizzaro reaction of B).

Step 2 (Chemical Equations): [1.5 Marks]

$$ \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_3 \xrightarrow{\text{CrO}_3, (\text{CH}_3\text{CO})_2\text{O}} \underbrace{\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}(\text{OCOCH}_3)_2}_{\text{A}} \xrightarrow{\text{H}_3\text{O}^+} \underbrace{\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CHO}}_{\text{B}} $$ $$ 2\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CHO} \xrightarrow{\text{Conc. NaOH}} \underbrace{\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{CH}_2\text{OH}}_{\text{C}} + \text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COONa} $$

Step 3 (Reasoning): [2 Marks]

  • (I): Carboxylic acids do not give carbonyl reactions because the lone pair on the oxygen atom of the $-\text{OH}$ group is involved in resonance with the carbonyl group, reducing the electrophilicity of the carbonyl carbon.
  • (II): Ethanoic acid is stronger because the carboxylate ion formed after losing a proton is stabilized by resonance, whereas the ethoxide ion formed from ethanol is destabilized by the electron-donating inductive effect of the alkyl group.

(b) (i) Products of Reactions

Step 1 (Reaction Completion): [1.5 Marks]

  • (I) $2\text{CH}_3\text{COOH} \xrightarrow[\text{heat}]{\text{P}_4\text{O}_{10}} (\text{CH}_3\text{CO})_2\text{O} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$ (Acetic anhydride)
  • (II) $\text{C}_6\text{H}_5\text{COCl} + (\text{CH}_3)_2\text{Cd} \rightarrow 2\text{CH}_3\text{COC}_6\text{H}_5 + \text{CdCl}_2$ (Acetophenone)
  • (III) Phthalamide $\xrightarrow{\text{strong heating}} \text{Phthalimide} + \text{NH}_3$
