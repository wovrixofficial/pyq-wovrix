(b) In normal adjustment, for a refracting telescope, the distance between objective and eye piece lens is 1.00 m. If the magnifying power of the telescope is 19, find the focal length of the objective and the eyepiece lens.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [0.5 Mark]

In normal adjustment, the final image is formed at infinity. The given parameters are:

  • Distance between objective and eyepiece, $L = f_o + f_e = 1.00\text{ m} = 100\text{ cm}$
  • Magnifying power, $M = \frac{f_o}{f_e} = 19$

The governing formulas are:

$$L = f_o + f_e$$ $$M = \frac{f_o}{f_e}$$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1.0 Mark]

From the magnifying power formula, we can express $f_o$ in terms of $f_e$:

$$f_o = 19 f_e$$

Substitute this into the length equation:

$$19 f_e + f_e = 100\text{ cm}$$ $$20 f_e = 100\text{ cm}$$ $$f_e = \frac{100}{20}\text{ cm} = 5\text{ cm}$$

Now, calculate $f_o$:

$$f_o = 19 \times 5\text{ cm} = 95\text{ cm}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The focal lengths are:

  • Focal length of the objective, $f_o = 95\text{ cm}$ (or $0.95\text{ m}$)
  • Focal length of the eyepiece, $f_e = 5\text{ cm}$ (or $0.05\text{ m}$)

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of a telescope like a giant funnel for light; the big objective lens gathers a huge amount of light and focuses it, while the tiny eyepiece acts like a magnifying glass to zoom in on that focused image so your eye can see the details clearly.
