Chemistry Coordination Compounds 4 Marks
Appeared in: 2025 (3 sets)
One of the most distinctive properties of transition metal complexes is their wide range of colours. This means that some of the visible spectrum is being removed from white light as it passes through the sample, so the light that emerges is no longer white. The colour of the complex is complementary to that which is absorbed. The complementary colour is the colour generated from the wavelength left over. If green light is absorbed by the complex, it appears red. Table given below, gives the relationship between different wavelengths of the light absorbed and the colour observed. (a) What is spectrochemical series ? Write the relationship between $\Delta_{\text{t}}$ and $\Delta_{\text{o}}$. (b) (i) Arrange $[\text{Co}(\text{CN})_6]^{3-}$, $[\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_6]^{3+}$ and $[\text{Cu}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4]^{2+}$ in increasing order of wavelength of light absorbed. OR (b) (ii) On the basis of crystal field theory, write the electronic configuration of $d^5$ ion if $\Delta_{\text{o}} < \text{P}$. (c) Define crystal field splitting energy.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

CBSE Class 12 Chemistry: Marking Scheme Solution

(a) Spectrochemical Series and Relationship between $\Delta_t$ and $\Delta_o$

  • Step 1 (Definition): [0.5 Mark] The spectrochemical series is an arrangement of ligands in order of increasing field strength (i.e., increasing crystal field splitting energy).
  • Step 2 (Relationship): [0.5 Mark] The relationship between tetrahedral splitting energy ($\Delta_t$) and octahedral splitting energy ($\Delta_o$) is given by: $$\Delta_t = \frac{4}{9} \Delta_o$$

(b) (i) Increasing order of wavelength of light absorbed

  • Step 1 (Concept): [0.5 Mark] The energy of absorbed light ($E$) is inversely proportional to the wavelength ($\lambda$): $E = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$. Stronger ligands cause larger splitting ($\Delta_o$), leading to the absorption of higher energy (shorter wavelength) light.
  • Step 2 (Analysis): [0.5 Mark] Based on the spectrochemical series, the field strength order is $\text{CN}^- > \text{NH}_3 > \text{H}_2\text{O}$. Thus, the splitting energy order is $[\text{Co}(\text{CN})_6]^{3-} > [\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_6]^{3+} > [\text{Cu}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4]^{2+}$.
  • Step 3 (Final Answer): [1 Mark] Since $\lambda \propto \frac{1}{\Delta_o}$, the increasing order of wavelength absorbed is: $$[\text{Co}(\text{CN})_6]^{3-} < [\text{Co}(\text{NH}_3)_6]^{3+} < [\text{Cu}(\text{H}_2\text{O})_4]^{2+}$$

OR (b) (ii) Electronic configuration of $d^5$ ion if $\Delta_o < P$

  • Step 1 (Condition): [0.5 Mark] When $\Delta_o < P$ (weak field ligand), the pairing energy ($P$) is greater than the splitting energy. Electrons prefer to occupy higher energy orbitals rather than pairing up.
  • Step 2 (Configuration): [1.5 Marks] The configuration is high-spin: $$t_{2g}^3 e_g^2$$

(c) Crystal Field Splitting Energy (CFSE)

  • Step 1 (Definition): [1 Mark] Crystal field splitting energy is the difference in energy between the two sets of $d$-orbitals ($t_{2g}$ and $e_g$ in octahedral complexes) resulting from the interaction between the metal ion $d$-orbitals and the surrounding ligand field.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the ligands as "pushers" that split the energy levels of the metal's electrons; strong ligands push hard, creating a huge energy gap (short wavelength light), while weak ligands push gently, creating a small gap (long wavelength light).
