(a) A parallel beam of light is incident on a face of a prism with refracting angle $60^{\circ}$. The angle of minimum deviation is found to be $30^{\circ}$. The refractive index of the material of the prism is close to : (A) $1\cdot3$ (B) $1\cdot4$ (C) $1\cdot5$ (D) $1\cdot6$ OR (b) The angle of minimum deviation for a ray of light incident on a thin prism, made of crown glass ($n = 1\cdot52$) is $D_{\text{m}}$. If the prism was made of dense flint glass ($n = 1\cdot62$) instead of crown glass, the angle of minimum deviation will : (A) decrease by $4%$ (B) increase by $4%$ (C) decrease by $19%$ (D) increase by $19%$
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution (a)

Step 1 (Formula & Given Data): [1/3 Mark]

The refractive index ($n$) of a prism in terms of the angle of prism ($A$) and the angle of minimum deviation ($\delta_m$) is given by the prism formula:

$$n = \frac{\sin\left(\frac{A + \delta_m}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)}$$

Given: $A = 60^{\circ}$, $\delta_m = 30^{\circ}$.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1/3 Mark]

Substituting the values into the formula:

$$n = \frac{\sin\left(\frac{60^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{60^{\circ}}{2}\right)} = \frac{\sin(45^{\circ})}{\sin(30^{\circ})}$$ $$n = \frac{1/\sqrt{2}}{1/2} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$$ $$n \approx 1.414$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1/3 Mark]

Comparing the result with the given options, $1.414$ is closest to $1.4$. Therefore, the correct option is (B).

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the refractive index as a measure of how much a prism "bends" light; since the light bends by a specific amount at a specific angle, we use the prism formula to calculate the exact material density required to cause that specific deflection.

Solution (b)

Step 1 (Formula & Given Data): [1/3 Mark]

For a thin prism, the angle of minimum deviation ($\delta_m$) is given by:

$$\delta_m = (n - 1)A$$

Given: $n_1 = 1.52$, $n_2 = 1.62$.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1/3 Mark]

Let $\delta_1 = (1.52 - 1)A = 0.52A$ and $\delta_2 = (1.62 - 1)A = 0.62A$.

The percentage increase is calculated as:

$$\text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \frac{\delta_2 - \delta_1}{\delta_1} \right) \times 100$$ $$\text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \frac{0.62A - 0.52A}{0.52A} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{0.10}{0.52} \right) \times 100 \approx 19.23\%$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1/3 Mark]

The value is approximately $19\%$. Since the refractive index increased, the deviation also increases. Therefore, the correct option is (D).

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: A thin prism acts like a simple wedge; if you make the material "thicker" or more refractive, it naturally forces the light to turn a sharper corner, increasing the deviation proportionally.
