(a) A plano-convex lens of refractive index $\mu_1$ is placed coaxially in contact with a biconcave lens of refractive index $\mu_2$ as shown in the figure. All curved faces are of radius of curvature $R$ each. Obtain the expression for the focal length of the combined lens. OR (b) A convex lens is kept coaxially on the left side of a concave mirror at a distance of $5f$ from it where $f$ is focal length of each of them. An object is kept at a distance of $2f$ on the left side of the convex lens. Draw the ray diagram showing the formation of the image by the combination. Find the distance of the final image from the mirror.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution for Part (a)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [0.5 Mark]

The focal length of a lens is given by the Lens Maker's Formula:

$$\frac{1}{f} = (\mu - 1) \left( \frac{1}{R_1} - \frac{1}{R_2} \right)$$

For the plano-convex lens ($L_1$): $R_1 = R$, $R_2 = \infty$.
For the biconcave lens ($L_2$): $R_1 = -R$, $R_2 = R$.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1.0 Mark]

Calculating focal length for $L_1$ ($f_1$):

$$\frac{1}{f_1} = (\mu_1 - 1) \left( \frac{1}{R} - \frac{1}{\infty} \right) = \frac{\mu_1 - 1}{R}$$

Calculating focal length for $L_2$ ($f_2$):

$$\frac{1}{f_2} = (\mu_2 - 1) \left( \frac{1}{-R} - \frac{1}{R} \right) = (\mu_2 - 1) \left( -\frac{2}{R} \right) = -\frac{2(\mu_2 - 1)}{R}$$

For lenses in contact, the equivalent focal length $F$ is:

$$\frac{1}{F} = \frac{1}{f_1} + \frac{1}{f_2}$$ $$\frac{1}{F} = \frac{\mu_1 - 1}{R} - \frac{2(\mu_2 - 1)}{R}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

$$\frac{1}{F} = \frac{\mu_1 - 2\mu_2 + 1}{R} \implies F = \frac{R}{\mu_1 - 2\mu_2 + 1}$$

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the lenses as "light benders." The first lens bends light inward to converge it, while the second lens (being concave) acts like a spreader; by adding their individual "bending powers" (reciprocal of focal length), we find the net effect on the light beam.

Solution for Part (b)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [0.5 Mark]

Lens Formula: $\frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$. Mirror Formula: $\frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$.
Given: $u_1 = -2f$, $f_1 = f$, distance between lens and mirror $d = 5f$.
