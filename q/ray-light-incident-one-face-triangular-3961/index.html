(a) A ray of light is incident on one face of a triangular glass prism of refractive index $n$ and is refracted out from the opposite face. Deduce the expression for refractive index of glass prism in terms of the angle of minimum deviation and angle of the prism. Write the condition on $n$ for refraction to take place on the opposite face. OR (b) State Huygens principle. Using it, draw a diagram and discuss the case of refraction of plane wave of light from a rarer medium to a denser medium at their plane interface. Hence derive Snell's law.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

(a) Refractive Index of a Triangular Prism

Step 1 (Governing Laws & Geometry): [1 Mark]

For a prism with angle $A$, angle of incidence $i$, angle of emergence $e$, and angle of deviation $\delta$, the following relations hold:

  • $A = r_1 + r_2$
  • $\delta = (i + r_1) + (e - r_2) = i + e - A$

At the condition of minimum deviation ($\delta = \delta_m$):

  • $i = e$
  • $r_1 = r_2 = r$

Step 2 (Mathematical Derivation): [3 Marks]

Substituting the minimum deviation conditions into the geometric relations:

$$A = r + r \implies r = \frac{A}{2}$$ $$\delta_m = i + i - A \implies i = \frac{A + \delta_m}{2}$$

Applying Snell's Law at the first interface:

$$n = \frac{\sin i}{\sin r}$$

Substituting the values of $i$ and $r$:

$$n = \frac{\sin\left(\frac{A + \delta_m}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right)}$$

Step 3 (Condition for Refraction & Final Answer): [1 Mark]

For refraction to take place at the second face, the angle of incidence at the second face ($r_2$) must be less than the critical angle ($C$). Since $r_2 = A/2$, the condition is:

$$\frac{A}{2} < C \implies \sin\left(\frac{A}{2}\right) < \frac{1}{n} \implies n < \frac{1}{\sin(A/2)}$$

(b) Huygens Principle and Snell's Law

Step 1 (Huygens Principle & Diagram Setup): [1 Mark]

Huygens Principle: Each point on a wavefront acts as a source of secondary wavelets, and the new wavefront is the forward envelope of these wavelets at a later time.

[Diagram Description: A plane wavefront $AB$ incident on interface $XY$. $A$ reaches the interface first, creating a wavelet of radius $v_2t$ in the denser medium while $B$ travels distance $v_1t$ to reach $B'$. The tangent from $B'$ to the wavelet at $A$ forms the refracted wavefront $A'B'$.]

Step 2 (Mathematical Derivation): [3 Marks]

In $\triangle AB'A'$ and $\triangle AB'B$:

  • $i = \angle BAB'$ (Angle of incidence)
  • $r = \angle ABA'$ (Angle of refraction)

From the geometry:

$$\sin i = \frac{BB'}{AB'} = \frac{v_1 t}{AB'}$$ $$\sin r = \frac{AA'}{AB'} = \frac{v_2 t}{AB'}$$

Dividing the two equations:

$$\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{v_1 t}{v_2 t} = \frac{v_1}{v_2}$$
