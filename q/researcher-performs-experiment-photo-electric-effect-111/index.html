Physics Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter 4 Marks
Appeared in: 2026 (3 sets)
A researcher performs an experiment on photo-electric effect using two metals A and B with unknown work functions. She illuminates the surfaces of A and B with monochromatic radiation of various frequencies and records the value of corresponding stopping potentials ($V_s$). The graph shows the variation of stopping potential ($V_s$) with the frequency of incident radiation ($\nu$) for metals A and B.
