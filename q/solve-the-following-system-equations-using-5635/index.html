Mathematics Matrices 5 Marks
Appeared in: 2024 (2 sets)
(a) Solve the following system of equations, using matrices : $\frac{2}{x} + \frac{3}{y} + \frac{10}{z} = 4, \frac{4}{x} - \frac{6}{y} + \frac{5}{z} = 1, \frac{6}{x} + \frac{9}{y} - \frac{20}{z} = 2$ where $x, y, z \neq 0$ OR (b) If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & \cot x \\ -\cot x & 1 \end{bmatrix}$, show that $A' A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -\cos 2x & -\sin 2x \\ \sin 2x & -\cos 2x \end{bmatrix}$.
