Physics Electromagnetic Induction 5 Marks
Appeared in: 2026 (2 sets)
OR (b) (i) State Lenz's law and explain that it follows the law of conservation of energy. (ii) Write the dimensional formula for self-inductance. The current in a coil changes from $8\cdot0\ \text{A}$ to $2\cdot0\ \text{A}$ in $0\cdot6\ \text{s}$. If an average emf induced in the coil is $50\ \text{V}$, calculate the self-inductance of the coil.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

(i) Lenz's Law and Conservation of Energy

Lenz's Law: The polarity of induced emf is such that it tends to produce a current which opposes the change in magnetic flux that produced it.

Conservation of Energy: When a magnet is moved towards a coil, the induced current creates a magnetic pole that repels the magnet. To overcome this repulsive force, external mechanical work must be done. This mechanical work is converted into electrical energy (induced current). If the induced current were to assist the change, it would violate the law of conservation of energy by creating energy out of nothing.

(ii) Dimensional Formula and Numerical Calculation

Step 1 (Dimensional Formula & Governing Law): [1 Mark]

  • Dimensional formula for self-inductance ($L$): $$[L] = [M^1 L^2 T^{-2} A^{-2}]$$
  • Governing formula for induced emf: $$|\varepsilon| = L \left| \frac{dI}{dt} \right|$$
  • Given data: $I_1 = 8.0\ \text{A}$, $I_2 = 2.0\ \text{A}$, $dt = 0.6\ \text{s}$, $\varepsilon = 50\ \text{V}$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [3 Marks]

  • Calculate the change in current ($dI$): $$dI = I_2 - I_1 = 2.0\ \text{A} - 8.0\ \text{A} = -6.0\ \text{A}$$ $$|dI| = 6.0\ \text{A}$$
  • Rearrange the formula to solve for $L$: $$L = \frac{|\varepsilon|}{|dI/dt|} = \frac{|\varepsilon| \cdot dt}{|dI|}$$
  • Substitute the values: $$L = \frac{50\ \text{V} \times 0.6\ \text{s}}{6.0\ \text{A}}$$
  • Perform the calculation: $$L = \frac{30}{6.0}\ \text{H}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1 Mark]

The self-inductance of the coil is: $$L = 5.0\ \text{H}$$

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of Lenz's Law like a "stubborn" person who always pushes back against any change you try to force on them; because they resist, you have to spend extra energy to move them, which is exactly where the electricity comes from!
