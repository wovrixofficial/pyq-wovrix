Appeared in: 2024 (3 sets)
The vectors $\vec{a} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}$, $\vec{b} = \hat{i} - 3\hat{j} - 5\hat{k}$ and $\vec{c} = -3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$ represents the sides of (A) an equilateral triangle (B) an obtuse-angled triangle (C) an isosceles triangle (D) a right-angled triangle
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1/4 Mark]

For three vectors $\vec{a}$, $\vec{b}$, and $\vec{c}$ to represent the sides of a triangle taken in order, their sum must be zero ($\vec{a} + \vec{b} + \vec{c} = \vec{0}$). To determine the type of triangle, we calculate the magnitudes of the sides and check the Pythagorean theorem ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$) or the dot product of the vectors.

Given vectors:

  • $\vec{a} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + \hat{k}$
  • $\vec{b} = \hat{i} - 3\hat{j} - 5\hat{k}$
  • $\vec{c} = -3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j} + 4\hat{k}$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1/2 Mark]

First, calculate the squares of the magnitudes of the sides:

$$|\vec{a}|^2 = 2^2 + (-1)^2 + 1^2 = 4 + 1 + 1 = 6$$ $$|\vec{b}|^2 = 1^2 + (-3)^2 + (-5)^2 = 1 + 9 + 25 = 35$$ $$|\vec{c}|^2 = (-3)^2 + 4^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 + 16 = 41$$

Observe the relationship between the magnitudes:

$$|\vec{a}|^2 + |\vec{b}|^2 = 6 + 35 = 41$$ $$|\vec{a}|^2 + |\vec{b}|^2 = |\vec{c}|^2$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1/4 Mark]

Since the sum of the squares of two sides equals the square of the third side, the vectors satisfy the Pythagorean theorem. Therefore, the vectors represent the sides of a right-angled triangle.

Correct Option: (D)

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of this like checking if a set of sticks can form a perfect corner; if the squares of the two shorter sides add up exactly to the square of the longest side, you've got a perfect 90-degree corner, which makes it a right-angled triangle!
