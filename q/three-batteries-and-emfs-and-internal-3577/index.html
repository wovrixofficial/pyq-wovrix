Physics Current Electricity 5 Marks
Appeared in: 2025 (3 sets)
(a) (i) Three batteries $E_1$, $E_2$ and $E_3$ of emfs and internal resistances ($4\text{ V}$, $2\text{ }\Omega$), ($2\text{ V}$, $4\text{ }\Omega$) and ($6\text{ V}$, $2\text{ }\Omega$) respectively are connected as shown in the figure. Find the values of the currents passing through batteries $E_1$, $E_2$ and $E_3$.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution: Kirchhoff’s Laws Application in Multi-Loop Circuits

Step 1 (Governing Laws & Circuit Setup): [1 Mark]

To solve for the currents, we apply Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL) at junction A and Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL) in the two independent loops. Let the currents through batteries $E_1$, $E_2$, and $E_3$ be $I_1$, $I_2$, and $I_3$ respectively. By KCL at junction A:

$$I_1 + I_2 = I_3$$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [3 Marks]

Applying KVL to the two loops (assuming clockwise direction):

  • Loop 1 (Left): Starting from point A, traversing through $E_1$ and $E_2$: $$4 - 2I_1 - 4I_2 - 2 = 0 \implies 2I_1 + 4I_2 = 2 \implies I_1 + 2I_2 = 1 \quad \text{---(Eq. 1)}$$
  • Loop 2 (Right): Traversing through $E_2$ and $E_3$: $$2 + 4I_2 - 2I_3 - 6 = 0 \implies 4I_2 - 2I_3 = 4 \implies 2I_2 - I_3 = 2 \quad \text{---(Eq. 2)}$$

Substitute $I_3 = I_1 + I_2$ into Eq. 2:

$$2I_2 - (I_1 + I_2) = 2 \implies -I_1 + I_2 = 2 \quad \text{---(Eq. 3)}$$

Adding Eq. 1 and Eq. 3:

$$(I_1 + 2I_2) + (-I_1 + I_2) = 1 + 2 \implies 3I_2 = 3 \implies I_2 = 1\text{ A}$$

Substituting $I_2 = 1$ into Eq. 3:

$$-I_1 + 1 = 2 \implies I_1 = -1\text{ A}$$

Calculating $I_3$:

$$I_3 = I_1 + I_2 = -1 + 1 = 0\text{ A}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1 Mark]

The currents flowing through the batteries are:

  • Current through $E_1$ ($I_1$): $-1\text{ A}$ (The negative sign indicates the current flows opposite to the assumed direction).
  • Current through $E_2$ ($I_2$): $1\text{ A}$.
  • Current through $E_3$ ($I_3$): $0\text{ A}$.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of Kirchhoff’s laws like a water plumbing system: KCL says the total water entering a pipe junction must equal the water leaving it, and KVL says that if you travel in a full circle around the pipes, the total "pressure" (voltage) you gain or lose must balance out to zero.
