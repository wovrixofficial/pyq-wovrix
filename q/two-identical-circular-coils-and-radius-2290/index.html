Physics Moving Charges and Magnetism 2 Marks
Appeared in: 2026 (2 sets)
Two identical circular coils A and B of radius $10\text{ cm}$ each are placed concentrically and mutually perpendicular in XY and YZ planes respectively. Currents of $1\text{ A}$ and $\sqrt{3}\text{ A}$ flow anticlockwise in the coils as seen from YZ plane. Find the magnitude of the net magnetic field at the common centre of the coils.
