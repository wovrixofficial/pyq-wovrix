Physics Electric Charges and Fields 3 Marks
Appeared in: 2025 (2 sets)
(a) Two small solid metal balls A and B of radii $R$ and $2R$ having charge densities $2\sigma$ and $3\sigma$ respectively are kept far apart. Find the charge densities on A and B after they are connected by a conducting wire. OR (b) Two infinitely long straight wires '1' and '2' are placed $d$ distance apart, parallel to each other, as shown in the figure. They are uniformly charged having charge densities $\lambda$ and $-\frac{\lambda}{2}$ respectively. Locate the position of the point from wire '1' at which the net electric field is zero and identify the region in which it lies.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution for Part (a)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

When two conductors are connected by a wire, charge flows until they reach the same electric potential ($V_A = V_B$). The potential of a spherical conductor is given by $V = \frac{kQ}{R}$.

Given:

  • Radius of A = $R$, Charge density = $2\sigma$
  • Radius of B = $2R$, Charge density = $3\sigma$
  • Initial charges: $Q_A = (2\sigma)(4\pi R^2) = 8\pi\sigma R^2$ and $Q_B = (3\sigma)(4\pi(2R)^2) = 48\pi\sigma R^2$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1.5 Marks]

Let the new charges be $Q_A'$ and $Q_B'$. By conservation of charge:

$$Q_A' + Q_B' = Q_A + Q_B = 8\pi\sigma R^2 + 48\pi\sigma R^2 = 56\pi\sigma R^2$$

Since potentials are equal ($V_A = V_B$):

$$\frac{k Q_A'}{R} = \frac{k Q_B'}{2R} \implies Q_B' = 2Q_A'$$

Substituting into the conservation equation:

$$Q_A' + 2Q_A' = 56\pi\sigma R^2 \implies 3Q_A' = 56\pi\sigma R^2 \implies Q_A' = \frac{56}{3}\pi\sigma R^2$$ $$Q_B' = 2 \times \frac{56}{3}\pi\sigma R^2 = \frac{112}{3}\pi\sigma R^2$$

New charge densities ($\sigma' = \frac{Q'}{4\pi r^2}$):

$$\sigma_A' = \frac{56\pi\sigma R^2}{3(4\pi R^2)} = \frac{14}{3}\sigma$$ $$\sigma_B' = \frac{112\pi\sigma R^2}{3(4\pi (2R)^2)} = \frac{112\pi\sigma R^2}{48\pi R^2} = \frac{7}{3}\sigma$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The new charge densities are $\sigma_A' = \frac{14}{3}\sigma$ and $\sigma_B' = \frac{7}{3}\sigma$.

Solution for Part (b)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [1 Mark]

The electric field due to an infinitely long wire is $E = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0 r}$. The net field is zero where the fields from both wires are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction.
