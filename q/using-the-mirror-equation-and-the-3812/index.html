Physics Ray Optics and Optical Instruments 2 Marks
Appeared in: 2025 (3 sets)
(a) Using the mirror equation and the formula of magnification, deduce that "the virtual image produced by a convex mirror is always diminished in size and is located between the pole and the focus." OR (b) A convex lens of focal length $10\text{ cm}$, a concave lens of focal length $15\text{ cm}$ and a third lens of unknown focal length are placed coaxially in contact. If the focal length of the combination is $+12\text{ cm}$, find the nature and focal length of the third lens, if all lenses are thin. Will the answer change if the lenses were thick ?
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution for (b)

Step 1 (Formula / Governing Law & Given Data): [0.5 Mark]

For a combination of thin lenses placed in contact, the equivalent focal length $F$ is given by the formula:

$$\frac{1}{F} = \frac{1}{f_1} + \frac{1}{f_2} + \frac{1}{f_3}$$

Given data (using sign convention):

  • $f_1 = +10\text{ cm}$ (Convex lens)
  • $f_2 = -15\text{ cm}$ (Concave lens)
  • $F = +12\text{ cm}$ (Combination)
  • $f_3 = ?$

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Mathematical Working): [1.0 Mark]

Substituting the values into the combination formula:

$$\frac{1}{12} = \frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{-15} + \frac{1}{f_3}$$

Calculating the sum of the first two terms:

$$\frac{1}{12} = \left( \frac{3 - 2}{30} \right) + \frac{1}{f_3}$$ $$\frac{1}{12} = \frac{1}{30} + \frac{1}{f_3}$$

Isolating $\frac{1}{f_3}$:

$$\frac{1}{f_3} = \frac{1}{12} - \frac{1}{30}$$ $$\frac{1}{f_3} = \frac{5 - 2}{60} = \frac{3}{60} = \frac{1}{20}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The focal length of the third lens is $f_3 = +20\text{ cm}$. Since the focal length is positive, the third lens is a convex lens.

Effect of thickness: Yes, the answer would change if the lenses were thick. The formula for the combination of thin lenses assumes the distance between them is negligible; for thick lenses, the separation distance and the principal planes must be considered, making the effective focal length different.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of lenses like filters for light; adding them together is like combining their powers. Because the third lens has a positive focal length, it acts as a "converging" lens that helps bring the light rays together to reach the desired total focus of $12\text{ cm}$.
