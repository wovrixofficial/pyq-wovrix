(a) (i) What are the two main considerations for designing the objective and eyepiece lenses of an astronomical telescope ? Obtain the expression for magnifying power of the telescope when the final image is formed at infinity. (ii) A ray of light is incident at an angle of $45^\circ$ at one face of an equilateral triangular prism and passes symmetrically through the prism. Calculate : (1) the angle of deviation produced by the prism (2) the refractive index of the material of the prism OR (b) (i) Describe a simple activity to observe diffraction pattern due to a single slit. (ii) The figure below shows an equiconvex lens (of refractive index $1.50$) in contact with a liquid layer on top of a plane mirror. A small needle with its tip on the principal axis is moved along the axis until its inverted image is found at the position of the needle. The distance of the needle from the lens is measured to be $45.0\text{ cm}$. When the liquid is removed and the experiment is repeated, the new distance is $30.0\text{ cm}$. Find the refractive index of the liquid.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Part (a)

(i) Considerations and Magnifying Power:

  • Considerations: (1) The objective lens should have a large aperture to increase light-gathering power and resolution. (2) The objective lens should have a large focal length to increase magnification.
  • Derivation: For an astronomical telescope in normal adjustment (final image at infinity), the magnifying power $m$ is defined as the ratio of the angle subtended by the image at the eye ($\beta$) to the angle subtended by the object at the objective ($\alpha$).

    • $$\tan \alpha \approx \alpha = \frac{h}{f_o}, \quad \tan \beta \approx \beta = \frac{h}{f_e}$$

    $$m = \frac{\beta}{\alpha} = \frac{h/f_e}{h/f_o} = -\frac{f_o}{f_e}$$

(ii) Prism Calculation:

Step 1 (Governing Laws): [0.5 Mark]

For symmetric passage, $i = e$ and $r_1 = r_2 = r = A/2$. Given $A = 60^\circ$ (equilateral), $i = 45^\circ$.

Step 2 (Mathematical Working): [1.0 Mark]

(1) Angle of deviation $\delta = i + e - A = 45^\circ + 45^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ$.

(2) Refractive index $\mu = \frac{\sin(\frac{A+\delta_m}{2})}{\sin(A/2)} = \frac{\sin(45^\circ)}{\sin(30^\circ)}$.

$$\mu = \frac{1/\sqrt{2}}{1/2} = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$$

Step 3 (Final Answer): [0.5 Mark]

$\delta = 30^\circ$ and $\mu = 1.414$.

Part (b)

(i) Diffraction Activity:

Place a razor blade edge near another to form a very narrow slit. Illuminate the slit with a monochromatic source (laser pointer). Observe the pattern on a distant screen: a bright central maximum flanked by alternating dark and weaker bright fringes.

(ii) Refractive Index of Liquid:

Step 1 (Governing Law): [1.0 Mark]

Lens Maker's Formula: $\frac{1}{f} = (\mu - 1)(\frac{1}{R_1} - \frac{1}{R_2})$. For equiconvex, $R_1 = R, R_2 = -R$.

Combined power $P = P_L + P_l$. Given $f_1 = 30\text{ cm}$ (lens), $f_{comb} = 45\text{ cm}$ (lens + liquid lens).

For the glass lens: $\frac{1}{30} = (1.5 - 1)(\frac{2}{R}) \implies \frac{1}{30} = 0.5 \times \frac{2}{R} \implies R = 30\text{ cm}$.
