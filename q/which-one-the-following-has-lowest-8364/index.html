Which one of the following has lowest $\text{pK}_a$ value ? (a) $\text{CH}_3 - \text{COOH}$ (b) $\text{O}_2\text{N} - \text{CH}_2 - \text{COOH}$ (c) $\text{Cl} - \text{CH}_2 - \text{COOH}$ (d) $\text{HCOOH}$
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Question: Which one of the following has lowest pKa value?

(a) CH3COOH, (b) O2NCH2COOH, (c) ClCH2COOH, (d) HCOOH

Step 1 (Governing Principle): [0.25 Mark]

The acidity of carboxylic acids is determined by the stability of the conjugate base (carboxylate ion). The relationship between acidity and pKa is inverse:

$$ \text{Acidity} \propto \frac{1}{\text{pK}_a} $$

Electron-Withdrawing Groups (EWG) stabilize the carboxylate anion through the inductive effect ($-I$ effect), thereby increasing acidity and decreasing the pKa value.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Comparative Analysis): [0.5 Mark]

  • (a) CH3COOH: Contains a methyl group ($+I$ effect), which destabilizes the carboxylate ion.
  • (d) HCOOH: Reference acid with no alkyl group.
  • (c) ClCH2COOH: Contains a Chlorine atom ($-I$ effect), which increases acidity compared to (a) and (d).
  • (b) O2NCH2COOH: Contains a Nitro group ($-NO_2$). The $-NO_2$ group exerts a much stronger electron-withdrawing ($-I$) effect than the Chlorine atom.

Comparing the strength of the $-I$ effect: $$-NO_2 > -Cl > -H > -CH_3$$ Therefore, the order of acidity is: $$\text{O}_2\text{NCH}_2\text{COOH} > \text{ClCH}_2\text{COOH} > \text{HCOOH} > \text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$$

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.25 Mark]

Since the pKa value is inversely proportional to acid strength, the compound with the strongest electron-withdrawing group will have the lowest pKa value.

Correct Option: (b) O2N - CH2 - COOH

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the carboxylate ion as a "hot potato" (negative charge) that needs to be cooled down; the $-NO_2$ group acts like a powerful vacuum cleaner that sucks away that extra charge, making the acid much stronger and its pKa value much smaller.
