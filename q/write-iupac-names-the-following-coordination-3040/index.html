Chemistry Coordination Compounds 2 Marks
Appeared in: 2025 (2 sets)
(a) Write IUPAC names of the following coordination compounds : (i) $\text{[CoCl}_2\text{(en)}_2\text{]SO}_4$ (ii) $\text{K}_3\text{[Fe(C}_2\text{O}_4)_3]$ OR (b) Differentiate between : (i) Double salt and Complex compound (ii) Didentate ligand and Ambidentate ligand
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution (a) IUPAC Nomenclature

Step 1 (Identification of Coordination Sphere and Oxidation State): [0.5 Mark]

To name coordination compounds, identify the ligands, their alphabetical order, and calculate the oxidation state of the central metal atom using the sum of charges equal to the net charge on the coordination entity.

Step 2 (Step-by-Step Calculation): [0.5 Mark]

  • (i) $\text{[CoCl}_2\text{(en)}_2\text{]SO}_4$:
    Let oxidation state of Co be $x$.
    $x + 2(-1) + 2(0) + (-2) = 0 \implies x - 4 = 0 \implies x = +3$.
    Ligands: Dichloro, bis(ethylenediamine). Alphabetical order: bis(ethylenediamine)dichloro.
  • (ii) $\text{K}_3\text{[Fe(C}_2\text{O}_4)_3]$:
    Let oxidation state of Fe be $x$.
    $3(+1) + x + 3(-2) = 0 \implies 3 + x - 6 = 0 \implies x = +3$.
    Ligand: Oxalato (anionic complex, so use 'ferrate').

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [1.0 Mark]

  • (i) Dichlorobis(ethane-1,2-diamine)cobalt(III) sulfate
  • (ii) Potassium trioxalatoferrate(III)

Solution (b) Differentiation

Step 1 (Definitions): [1.0 Mark]

  • (i) Double Salt vs Complex Compound: A double salt (e.g., Mohr's salt) dissociates completely into simple ions in water, whereas a complex compound (e.g., $\text{K}_4\text{[Fe(CN)}_6\text{]}$) retains its identity and does not dissociate into simple ions.
  • (ii) Didentate vs Ambidentate Ligand: A didentate ligand has two donor atoms that bind simultaneously to the metal, while an ambidentate ligand has two potential donor atoms but binds through only one at a time.

Step 2 (Comparative Analysis): [0.5 Mark]

  • Double salts lose their properties in solution; complexes retain them.
  • Didentate (e.g., $\text{en}$) forms a chelate ring; Ambidentate (e.g., $\text{SCN}^-$) shows linkage isomerism.

Step 3 (Final Concluded Answer): [0.5 Mark]

The distinction lies in the stability of the coordination entity in solution for (i) and the mode of attachment to the central metal ion for (ii).

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of a double salt like a friendship that breaks up as soon as you enter a room (water), while a complex compound is like a tight-knit team that stays together no matter where they go!
