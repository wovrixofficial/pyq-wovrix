Chemistry Coordination Compounds 2 Marks
Appeared in: 2025 (3 sets)
(a) Write the IUPAC name of the complex $[ ext{Pt}( ext{en})_2 ext{Cl}_2]^{2+}$. Draw the structure of geometrical isomer of this complex which is optically inactive. OR (b) (i) Write the formula of the following coordination compound : Pentaamminecarbonatocobalt(III)chloride (ii) Write the IUPAC name of the linkage isomer of the complex $[ ext{Co}( ext{NH}_3)_5( ext{NO}_2)] ext{Cl}_2$.
✓ Verified CBSE Marking Scheme Official Evaluation Steps & EL10 Insight

Solution for Coordination Compounds (CBSE Class 12 Chemistry)

(a) IUPAC Name and Geometrical Isomer

  • Step 1 (IUPAC Nomenclature): [0.5 Mark]

    Identify the ligands and central metal oxidation state. The complex is $[Pt(en)_2Cl_2]^{2+}$.

    Oxidation state of Pt: $x + 2(0) + 2(-1) = +2 \implies x = +4$.

    IUPAC Name: Dichloridobis(ethane-1,2-diamine)platinum(IV) ion.

  • Step 2 (Geometrical Isomerism): [0.5 Mark]

    The optically inactive isomer is the trans-isomer, as it possesses a plane of symmetry.

  • Step 3 (Structure): [1.0 Mark]

    Drawing the trans-isomer (Cl atoms at 180°):

    $$ \begin{array}{c} \text{Cl} \\ | \\ \text{en} - \text{Pt} - \text{en} \\ | \\ \text{Cl} \end{array} $$

OR (b) Formula and Linkage Isomer

  • Step 1 (Formula Construction): [1.0 Mark]

    Pentaamminecarbonatocobalt(III) chloride:

    Pentaammine = $(NH_3)_5$; Carbonato = $(CO_3)^{2-}$; Cobalt(III) = $Co^{3+}$; Chloride = $Cl^-$.

    Balancing charges: $[Co(NH_3)_5(CO_3)]Cl$. Since $Co$ is $+3$ and $CO_3$ is $-2$, the complex ion is $+1$, requiring one $Cl^-$.

    Final Formula: $[Co(NH_3)_5(CO_3)]Cl$.

  • Step 2 (Linkage Isomerism): [1.0 Mark]

    The complex is $[Co(NH_3)_5(NO_2)]Cl_2$. The linkage isomer involves the ambidentate ligand $NO_2^-$ bonding through oxygen ($ONO^-$).

    IUPAC Name: Pentaamminenitrito-O-cobalt(III) chloride.

💡 EL10 Concept Insight: Think of the trans isomer like a sandwich where the two identical 'Cl' toppings are on the exact opposite sides, making the whole thing look perfectly balanced and symmetrical (optically inactive). For linkage isomers, think of the $NO_2$ group as a person with two hands; it can hold the metal with its 'Nitrogen hand' (nitro) or its 'Oxygen hand' (nitrito), changing the name but keeping the same atoms!
