STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

Academic Fair Dealing & Copyright Policy

Statutory Basis: Reproduction of examination questions for educational practice and evaluation is protected under Section 52(1)(a) and Section 52(1)(h) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957.

1. Educational Fair Dealing Statement

PYQ Wovrix is a non-commercial educational study reference service. Questions compiled on this platform represent historical public examinations administered by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and the National Testing Agency (NTA).

Under Section 52(1)(h) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the reproduction of any work as part of questions to be answered in an examination, or in answers to such questions, does NOT constitute an infringement of copyright. Furthermore, Section 52(1)(a) grants explicit statutory immunity for fair dealing in research, private study, criticism, and review.

2. Attribution of Official Authorities

All question papers, marks rubrics, and answer keys are the intellectual property of their respective governing authorities:

  • CBSE: Central Board of Secondary Education, Shiksha Kendra, Delhi, India.
  • NTA: National Testing Agency, Ministry of Education, Government of India.

PYQ Wovrix is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with CBSE or NTA.

3. Formal Notice and Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative thereof and believe that any material available on this website infringes upon your copyright outside the statutory scope of fair dealing, please provide a written communication to our Designated Grievance Officer:

  • Identification of Work: The specific Question ID, URL, or excerpt alleged to be infringed.
  • Proof of Authority: Evidence demonstrating legal standing or authorization from the copyright holder.
  • Contact Information: Valid email, address, and telephone number.
  • Good Faith Statement: A declaration that the claim is made in good faith and that the information provided is accurate under penalty of perjury.

Notices may be submitted directly to: legal@wovrix.in. Upon receipt of a valid notice, we promptly review and disable access within 48 business hours.
