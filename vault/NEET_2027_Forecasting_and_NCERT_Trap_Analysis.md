## PART 2: Line-by-Line NCERT Anchoring & Distractor Trap Analysis

NTA question authors engineer distractors using systematic student misconceptions. Below are detailed forensic autopsies of signature questions across the archive:


### Hydrogen Spectral Series Transitions • Physics (`NEET_2024_Q026`)

**Exam Question**: Match List I (Spectral Lines of Hydrogen) with List II (Wavelengths):
A. $n_2=3 \to n_1=2$ (Balmer $H_\alpha$)
B. $n_2=4 \to n_1=2$ (Balmer $H_\beta$)
C. $n_2=5 \to n_1=2$ (Balmer $H_\gamma$)
D. $n_2=6 \to n_1=2$ (Balmer $H_\delta$)
Wavelengths: I. 410.2 nm, II. 434.1 nm, III. 656.3 nm, IV. 486.1 nm

- **Exact NCERT Anchor**: `NCERT Class 12 Physics, Part II, Chapter 12 (Atoms), Page 429, Table 12.1`
- **Official Answer**: **A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I**

**Forensic Distractor & Psychology Breakdown**:

* **Correct Logic**: Energy $\Delta E = 13.6 \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$. As $n_2$ increases, energy gap increases, so wavelength $\lambda = \frac{hc}{\Delta E}$ must decrease monotonically: $3\to 2$ gives largest $\lambda$ (656.3 nm), while $6\to 2$ gives smallest $\lambda$ (410.2 nm).
* **Distractor Trap A (Inverse Logic)**: Students often invert the relation, falsely assuming higher initial orbit $n_2$ means higher wavelength. Option (A-I, B-II, C-IV, D-III) directly exploits this.
* **Distractor Trap B (Direct Calculation Trap)**: Students who attempt to calculate Rydberg's formula $\frac{1}{\lambda} = R \left(\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{9}\right)$ waste 3+ minutes and make numerical decimal arithmetic errors instead of applying the monotonic ordering shortcut.

### Gauss's Law Net Flux Surface Integral • Physics (`NEET_2023_Q005`)

**Exam Question**: If $\oint_S \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{S} = 0$ over a closed surface, then:
(1) The number of flux lines entering must equal number of flux lines leaving
(2) The magnitude of electric field on surface is constant
(3) All charges must necessarily be inside surface
(4) Electric field inside surface is necessarily uniform

- **Exact NCERT Anchor**: `NCERT Class 12 Physics, Part I, Chapter 1 (Electric Charges and Fields), Page 34, Section 1.14`
- **Official Answer**: **Option (1)**

**Forensic Distractor & Psychology Breakdown**:

* **Correct Logic**: $\Phi_{net} = \oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{S} = \frac{q_{enc}}{\varepsilon_0} = 0$ implies net charge enclosed is zero. Flux entering is negative, flux leaving is positive; sum is zero only if entering equals leaving.
* **Distractor Trap (Option 2)**: Confuses zero flux with electrostatic shielding or equipotential surface.
* **Distractor Trap (Option 3 & 4)**: Candidates confuse $\oint \vec{E} \cdot d\vec{S} = 0$ with $\vec{E} = 0$. Dipoles inside the surface produce non-zero, non-uniform fields everywhere, yet net flux is strictly zero.

### Faraday's Electrolysis Mol of Electrons • Chemistry (`NEET_2024_Q064`)

**Exam Question**: Match List I with List II:
A. $1\text{ mol of } \text{H}_2\text{O to } \text{O}_2$
B. $1\text{ mol of } \text{MnO}_4^- \text{ to } \text{Mn}^{2+}$
C. $1.5\text{ mol of } \text{Ca from molten } \text{CaCl}_2$
D. $1\text{ mol of } \text{FeO to } \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$
Charge: I. 2F, II. 5F, III. 3F, IV. 1F

- **Exact NCERT Anchor**: `NCERT Class 12 Chemistry, Part I, Chapter 3 (Electrochemistry), Page 85, In-text Question 3.10`
- **Official Answer**: **A-I, B-II, C-III, D-IV**

**Forensic Distractor & Psychology Breakdown**:

* **Correct Logic**: For A: $2\text{H}_2\text{O} \to \text{O}_2 + 4\text{H}^+ + 4e^- \implies 1\text{ mol }\text{H}_2\text{O}$ requires $2F$. For B: $\text{Mn}^{+7} + 5e^- \to \text{Mn}^{2+} \implies 5F$. For C: $\text{Ca}^{2+} + 2e^- \to \text{Ca}$, so $1.5\text{ mol} \times 2 = 3F$. For D: $2\text{FeO} \to \text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3$ oxidation state goes from $+2$ to $+3$ ($1e^-$ per iron) $\implies 1F$.
* **Distractor Trap (Water Oxidation)**: Students frequently memorize $4F$ for oxygen evolution without noticing the question specifies *1 mole of water*, which yields only 0.5 mol $\text{O}_2$ and requires $2F$ instead of $4F$.

### Pleiotropism Definition • Botany (`NEET_2023_Q105`)

**Exam Question**: The phenomenon of pleiotropism refers to:
(1) Presence of several alleles of a single gene controlling a single crossover
(2) Presence of two alleles, each of the two genes controlling a single trait
(3) A single gene affecting multiple phenotypic expression
(4) More than two genes affecting a single character

- **Exact NCERT Anchor**: `NCERT Class 12 Biology, Chapter 5 (Principles of Inheritance and Variation), Page 85, Section 5.4`
- **Official Answer**: **Option (3)**

**Forensic Distractor & Psychology Breakdown**:

* **Correct Logic**: Direct verbatim definition from NCERT Section 5.4: 'A single gene can exhibit multiple phenotypic expression, such a gene is called a pleiotropic gene' (e.g. Phenylketonuria).
* **Distractor Trap (Option 4)**: Option 4 defines Polygenic Inheritance (e.g., human skin color, height). NTA deliberately pairs Pleiotropy and Polygenic Inheritance side-by-side because students constantly reverse 'one gene $\to$ many traits' versus 'many genes $\to$ one trait'.

### Eutrophication & Biomagnification Falsehoods • Botany (`NEET_2023_Q136`)

**Exam Question**: Which one of the following statements is NOT correct?
(1) Micro-organisms consuming oxygen in sewage polluted water cause death of aquatic organisms
(2) Algal blooms caused by excess organic matter in water improve water quality and promote fisheries
(3) Water hyacinth grows abundantly in eutrophic water bodies and leads to imbalance
(4) Amount of some toxic substances increases at successive trophic levels

- **Exact NCERT Anchor**: `NCERT Class 12 Biology, Chapter 16 (Environmental Issues), Page 276, Section 16.2.2`
- **Official Answer**: **Option (2)**

**Forensic Distractor & Psychology Breakdown**:

* **Correct Logic**: Algal blooms cause deterioration of water quality and fish mortality (choking oxygen), rather than improving water quality or promoting fisheries.
* **Distractor Trap (Negative Stem 'NOT')**: NTA's highest casualty stem format is 'NOT correct'. Under exam pressure, students read Option 1, see it is factually true, and mark it immediately without reading Option 2 or recognizing the negative qualifier.
